Who We Are

Noshimza Corner is a food restaurant focused on providing customers with tasty meals, good service and a welcoming environment. We believe that food is more than just something to eat. It is also a way for people to come together and enjoy good moments.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality food and friendly service while making sure that our customers have an enjoyable dining experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted restaurant known for good food, affordable prices and excellent customer service.

What We Offer

  • Breakfast meals
  • Lunch meals
  • Dinner meals
  • Snacks
  • Cold drinks
  • Take-away meals

Our Customers

We welcome everyone, including families, students, workers, friends and visitors who are looking for a good meal in a relaxed environment.
